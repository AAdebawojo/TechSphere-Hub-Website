Meet our founders

People behind the purpose.

Co-founder

Ayomikun Adebawojo

Ayomikun Adebawojo is the Co-founder of TechSphere Hub and a passionate data enthusiast dedicated to shaping the next generation of data analysts. With over five years of hands-on experience, he works as a Power BI Developer, specializing in transforming complex datasets into actionable insights that drive business success.

Holding a background in Economics and credentials as a Chartered Accountant, Ayomikun blends financial expertise with advanced analytics, delivering high-quality solutions and fostering impactful learning experiences.

Co-founder

Oluwatobi Adeolu Adetunji

Oluwatobi Adeolu Adetunji is a versatile Marketing Data Analyst with over five years of experience in leveraging data to drive business growth and optimize operations. Formerly a Digital Marketing Manager, Tobi expertly combines marketing knowledge with data analytics across diverse industries.

Currently at SiNet Technologies, he uses tools like Excel, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI to elevate marketing strategies. As Co-founder of TechSphere Hub, Tobi is passionate about building a vibrant community of tech professionals and fostering collaboration in the tech ecosystem.
